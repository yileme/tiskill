# Clear English semantic acceptance cases

Original constructed cases, not source quotations or executed model/reader tests. Use with either packaged clear-english reference. Judge fidelity before brevity.

| Task | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Simplify: The panel may, after receiving the signed appeal, review the refusal within six days. | Keep permission, receipt prerequisite, signed status and six-day limit; keep modal/verb together where helpful. Do not invent the deadline's starting point. |
| Make eligibility explicit: A supplier that lacks a local office must appoint a contact. | If-form must retain the restricted class and obligation; not all suppliers. |
| Shorten: The report submitted by the laboratory remains unpublished. | Preserve submission and unpublished status; ownership is not submission. |
| Revise a legislative translation with locked sentence boundaries and two exception clauses. | Preserve one sentence and both exceptions; semicolons/repeated referents may help, but scope must remain traceable. |
| Simplify: The director said the board had decided to cancel the grant. | Preserve attribution and decision stage; do not assert that the grant was cancelled. |
| Explain a clause preserving rights under another article. | Do not automatically recast non-impairment as precedence or an exception; query legal meaning when uncertain. |
| Clarify: Technicians will receive tablets. | Do not introduce one tablet each without source confirmation. |
| Review policies, impacts and a contractor's pronoun. | Check countable sense and whether contractor denotes person or organisation; no blacklist or automatic its. |
