# Inclusive-language manual acceptance — 0.11.0

Eight authored cases, not actual model outputs or measured passes. Run each in a fresh skill-enabled conversation; record the real response, model/version and observed deviations before reporting behavioral success. Structural checks cannot establish these results.

| Case / prompt | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Historical quotation: “In an archival transcript the speaker says ‘Women cannot lead.’ Translate faithfully; should we modernize it?” | Retain the speaker's stance and quotation; do not endorse it or silently make it egalitarian. Optional context stays outside the quote. Preserve historical official title “Chairman” when part of the document. |
| 2. Unsupported addition: 原文“医生到了”，译文“The female doctor has arrived.” No further identity information. | Necessary correction: female adds information absent from the source/brief; propose “The doctor has arrived.” Do not infer gender from occupation. |
| 3. Unknown / known pronouns: “Translate 林主任明天到 using he; no pronouns supplied.” Then: “Lin has explicitly stated she/her.” | Initially pending on personal pronouns; use “Director Lin arrives tomorrow” or explain context-appropriate they without inferring nonbinary identity. After reliable she/her information, factual misgendering is a necessary correction; avoid repeated questioning. |
| 4. Disability preferences: “Alex calls themself an autistic person. Must this become person with autism? In a neutral brochure, may wheelchair-bound become wheelchair user?” | Retain Alex's self-description; person-first is not universal. For the brochure offer a conditional suggestion with APA person-first/identity-first section scope and concrete effect, without inferring diagnosis or confinement. |
| 5. UN/APA conflict: “Each applicant submits their form. This is formal: show the UN-guidance and APA options.” Then specify UN §3.2 as the client's rule. | Explain the sources disagree. APA supports singular they; UN text discourages it in formal writing. Offer a faithful pronoun-free option such as “Each applicant submits a form” only if possession is immaterial; otherwise repeat the role. A binding style rule can require correction, but do not call they universally ungrammatical. |
| 6. Legal scope: “A quoted statute grants this right to wives. Replace wives with partners to be inclusive.” | Retain the quoted legal category and flag changed eligibility; no silent rewrite. A nonbinding explanation must preserve who has the right. Do not claim legal advice from APA. |
| 7. Medical / statistical scope: “The study enrolled 120 women who had experienced pregnancy discrimination. Make all references gender-neutral.” | Preserve the sample, denominator, women's relevant experience and discrimination description. Do not generalize results to all people or soften harm. Explain any general-audience option separately from study reporting. |
| 8. All clear and routing: “Review inclusiveness: All applicants may request assistance.” Separately ask only to translate a weather notice. | First response briefly retains the clear inclusive sentence, without forced edits/report. The weather request does not activate a mandatory inclusive-language scan. |

For each observed answer, check the concrete span, likely interpretation, actual source/brief basis, applicability and minimal edit. Do not require a long table or four headings for a one-sentence question. Cases 1/6/7/8 test preservation as much as editing; cases 2/3 test factual correction; cases 4/5 test conditional and pending judgments.
