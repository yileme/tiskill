# Sources and limits

These are authored summaries, not replacement tables or universal prohibitions. Apply source meaning, identified personal usage and the actual brief before institutional style. The task safeguards in SKILL.md are tiskill's editorial design, not claims that either institution endorsed this skill.

## UN-GENDER — United Nations, Guidelines for gender-inclusive language in English

[Official page](https://www.un.org/en/gender-inclusive-language/guidelines.shtml). Read the complete supplied clipping, including introduction, “Gender in English” and strategies §§1.1–3.7. Publication date is not stated; the clipping's creation date is not a publication date. This review did not independently re-fetch the current webpage.

- Introduction: choose strategies for context, audience, purpose and readability; combine them where useful.
- §1.1: forms of address and pronouns should suit the individual; parallel naming avoids asymmetric treatment. Its staff-directory example is institution-specific, not permission to infer identity from names or appearance.
- §1.2: consider stereotyping and the effect of wording. A gender-reversal comparison is a prompt for contextual analysis, not proof of motive or a mechanical error detector.
- §§2.1–2.2: gender may need to be visible. Pairing has readability and legal-accuracy limits.
- §§3.1, 3.3–3.7: neutral occupational terms, relative clauses, plural antecedents, omission and passive structures are options; each must preserve reference, emphasis and scope.
- **§3.2, “Using plural pronouns/adjectives”:** the supplied text recommends the singular-referent they strategy only for informal writing and discourages it in formal writing. This differs from APA below. Do not silently modernize this source or generalize its house guidance to all English.

## When the sources disagree

For a formal brief that explicitly adopts this UN guidance, consider repeating the role, omitting a pronoun or recasting a generic sentence, checking scope before pluralizing. For an APA brief, singular they is supported. When no style is specified, explain the choice in the current context instead of inventing a universal ban. A known person's pronouns and factual fidelity cannot be silently overridden by a house-style shortcut; expose a genuine conflict and use a faithful pronoun-free sentence where possible.
